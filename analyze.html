Backtest Analyzer v0.3

Find structural weaknesses in an MT5 backtest: outlier dependence, hidden tail risk, cost drag, floating drawdown. Files are processed in your browser and never uploaded.

1. Import backtest data

Accepted: a closed-trade or deals CSV (needs a Profit column; Commission, Swap, Type, Symbol and Time are used when present) or a Strategy Tester graph export (DATE, BALANCE, EQUITY, DEPOSIT LOAD). UTF-16 files from MT5 are supported.

5. Behaviour breakdown

Look for groups that carry the whole result or consistently lose. Small groups are noisy.

6. Monte Carlo resampling

Re-orders and resamples your trades to show how much of the result and the drawdown depends on the luck of the sequence. Needs at least 20 trades. Trades are treated as independent, so real risk is usually higher than shown. Results are added to the downloaded report.

7. Time stability and market regimes

Does the result hold across calendar months, and across market conditions? Month results come from the trade timestamps. Market regimes need prices, which a trade list does not contain, so they are optional (below).

Market regimes (optional)

Upload bars for the traded symbol: in MT5 open Symbols, go to Bars and export bars to CSV (menu names vary by build). Each trade is assigned the regime measured on bars completed before it opened, with trend and volatility each split into thirds. Results are added to the downloaded report.

8. Trading sessions

Net result by approximate trading session, based on open time. Broker servers usually run on UTC+2 in winter and UTC+3 in summer; set a fixed offset if yours differs. A wrong offset shifts trades between sessions and can split a pattern across two of them; the "By hour" table in section 5 does not depend on the offset.
